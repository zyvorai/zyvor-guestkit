# GuestKit: support subscriptions

GuestKit is source-available under the Zyvor Production Licence v2.0. A 30-day trial is available on request from sales@zyvor.dev (a paid evaluation Order at no charge); production and any other use needs a paid Order under the Zyvor Production Licence v2.0.

Teams that want supported releases and defined response targets can buy an annual **support subscription** from Zyvor.

| Plan | What you get | Coverage |
|------|--------------|----------|
| Standard | Deployment and upgrade guidance, defined supported configuration | Business hours |
| Supported | Standard plus priority queue and incident triage | Business hours, faster response |
| Enterprise | Supported plus a named technical contact and 24x7 critical intake | One-hour initial response target for critical issues |

Support is priced on the one **Zyvor Support** meter: per node per year, covering GuestKit and all Zyvor infrastructure software on that node. It is never stacked per product, so a node that already holds Zyvor Support does not pay again. **Supported:** INR 59,999 per node per year including 18% GST in India (USD 600 excluding tax elsewhere), minimum 5 nodes (INR 299,995 / USD 3,000). **Supported Plus** (adds 24x7 Severity 1 and 2, quarterly reviews and upgrade assistance): INR 119,999 per node per year including GST (USD 1,200), minimum 5 nodes (INR 599,995 / USD 6,000). Volume: 256-499 nodes -10%, 500-999 -15%, 1,000+ -20%; multi-year: 2 years -5%, 3 years -10%; volume and multi-year together are capped at 25%. Minimums are annual contract values and apply after discounts. Production use needs a paid Order. Production licences of other Zyvor products come with a 30-day trial key.

Prices: INR incl. 18% GST in India; US dollars excl. tax elsewhere (VAT, GST or sales tax added where applicable). Pay online by UPI, card or netbanking where a buy page exists; international cards work (charged in rupees); or request a US-dollar invoice by bank transfer. Custom pricing: contact [sales@zyvor.dev](mailto:sales@zyvor.dev). Buy / get a quote: <https://zyvor.dev/buy>.

## Terms

- Annual prepaid subscription is the default, with a 12-month minimum. Multi-year prepay is available.
- Minimums apply after any discounts.
- Implementation, migration, training and managed operations are separate services.
- At expiry, updates, downloads and support end. Rights under the Zyvor Production Licence v2.0 continue as stated in the license.
- Response targets need a contracted scope and are not uptime guarantees.
- Existing signed agreements keep their agreed terms.
